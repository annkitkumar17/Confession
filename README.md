# ✨ A Birthday Love Letter For Chaya

A bespoke, editorial, mobile-first birthday surprise website crafted with intimacy, warmth, and interactive storytelling by **Harsh** for **Chaya**.

---

## 🌹 Experience Overview (4 Chapters)

Each chapter is **strictly interaction-gated**: Chaya cannot scroll ahead or skip chapters until she interacts with the current page.

1. **Chapter 1: The Envelope Opening**
   - 3D tactile sealed envelope addressed to **Chaya** with a deep burgundy wax seal (`#6E2B3B`) and embossed "C" monogram.
   - **Interaction:** Tapping the seal or envelope unfolds the top flap in 3D, breaks the seal, triggers soft confetti, and smoothly unlocks and transitions to Chapter 2.

2. **Chapter 2: The Question & The Playful "No"**
   - High-contrast serif question: *"Before I wish you happy birthday… Will you let me celebrate you today?"*
   - **Playful runaway "No" button:** When Chaya tries to hover or tap "No", it dodges across the screen, rotates playfully, shrinks, and displays cheeky banter (e.g. *"No? Bold choice, Chaya."*, *"Rude. Try again."*, *"The universe strongly recommends Yes."*).
   - **The "Yes, obviously" button:** Grows larger, bolder, and glows with increasing confidence.
   - **Interaction:** Only clicking *"Yes, obviously"* unlocks and glides into Chapter 3.

3. **Chapter 3: The Birthday Letter**
   - Typeset like a luxury invitation on warm cotton deckle-edge paper with a postage seal detail.
   - Beautiful, emotionally grounded message for Chaya:
     > *"Happy Birthday, Chaya.*
     > *I hope today feels as special as you make ordinary days feel.*
     > *Thank you for your laughter, your kindness, your chaos, and the way you make everything feel a little lighter.*
     > *I hope this year brings you everything you quietly wish for—and a few beautiful surprises too.*
     > *With too much love,*
     > *Harsh"*
   - **Interaction:** Tapping the glowing button at the bottom (*"One last surprise for you ✨"*) unlocks and opens Chapter 4.

4. **Chapter 4: Final Celebration & Candle Wish**
   - Full-screen finale with an interactive birthday candle with a flickering flame.
   - **Interaction:** Tapping the flame or clicking *"Make a wish ✨"*:
     - Blows out the candle flame with realistic rising smoke.
     - Launches an elegant multi-burst confetti celebration in warm ivory, rose, deep burgundy, and gold.
     - Plays celestial music-box chimes.
     - Reveals the promise: *"Wish granted. I’ll be here for all of them. May this year be the softest, brightest, most unforgettable chapter yet."*
   - Thoughtful footer: *"Made with love, bad jokes, and a little too much thought. — Harsh"*

## 🎨 Design System

- **Background:** Warm Ivory (`#FFF8F3`) with subtle tactile film grain overlay.
- **Primary Accent:** Soft Rose Pink (`#E8747F`)
- **Deep Accent:** Burgundy / Plum (`#6E2B3B`)
- **Text:** Warm Charcoal (`#2E222A`)
- **Typography:**
  - Display: `Fraunces` (Editorial high-contrast serif)
  - Body: `Plus Jakarta Sans` (Clean modern sans)
  - Accent: `Caveat` (Authentic handwritten script)
- **Audio:** Custom Web Audio API acoustic music-box synthesizer (muted by default to respect privacy).

## 🚀 How to Run & View

```bash
npm run dev
```
Open **[http://localhost:3000/](http://localhost:3000/)** in your browser.
